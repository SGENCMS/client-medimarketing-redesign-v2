The 2 KPIs that rule them all

Two numbers decide who gets the adjustment.

Someone whose lower back just locked up does not compare logos. They book the first clinic Google shows whose page actually opens. We are judged on those two moments, not on impressions.

KPI 01 · Speed

FCPFirst Contentful Paint

The gap between a tap on your listing and the first thing a hurting patient sees. 85% of traffic globally is from a mobile device, and nobody with a stiff neck waits on a blank phone screen. They tap back and choose another chiropractor.

Between a search and a booked first visit, sites we build on SGEN CMS paint in 0.9s on desktop. A slow competitor site we measured took 7.8s.
KPI 02 · Visibility

SoLVShare of Local Visibility

The part of your service radius where your clinic sits in Google's local top-3 for "chiropractor near me" and for the help people look for with back pain, neck pain or injury recovery. We measure it point by point across the map.

In the local pack, position 1 takes 18% of clicks. A clinic ranked past the top-3 gets under 1%.
