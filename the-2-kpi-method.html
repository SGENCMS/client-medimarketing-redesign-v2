The two numbers

Two numbers stand between a search and a booked patient.

Before a patient can book, two things have to happen. They have to find your practice in Google's local results, and the page they tap has to show up. SoLV measures the first, FCP measures the second, and a slow first paint holds back every other form of digital marketing you pay for.

KPI 1 · The page

FCPFirst Contentful Paint

The time between a tap on your link and the moment the browser shows the first text or image of your page.

Why it counts: it is the first reason a visitor leaves a website.
KPI 2 · The map

SoLVShare of Local Visibility

How often your Google Business listing is found in the top 3 of Google's local results, measured across the area you serve.

How it is scored: the share of scan pins where your listing holds a top-3 spot.
KPI 1 · First Contentful Paint

A tap on a result, then a white screen.

A patient searches, picks a practice from the local list and taps. Then the wait begins. First Contentful Paint is the length of that wait: the time until the browser paints the first text or image of the page. Until it does, the patient sees nothing, and nothing on the page can persuade them to stay.

FCP = bounce rate

Each extra second raises the bounce rate.

A bounce is a visitor who leaves from the page they landed on without going any further. Our shorthand is FCP = bounce rate: the slower the first paint, the more visitors bounce, and the ladder shows by how much.

FCP = bounce rate Added bounce rate at each First Contentful Paint time.
3 seconds+20%
4 seconds+40%
5 seconds+60%
85% of traffic globally is from a mobile device. That makes mobile FCP the most important KPI when the aim is to turn traffic into transactions.
What good looks like

Google's marks, and where our sites land.

Google publishes marks for First Contentful Paint: 1.8 seconds or less is good, and more than 3.0 seconds is poor. We check FCP with Google PageSpeed Insights before we touch a site and again after. The sites we build on SGEN CMS, our own platform, paint in 0.9 seconds on desktop.

KPI 2 · Share of Local Visibility

One Local Radius Scan, three measures.

SoLV is how often your Google Business listing is found in the top 3 on Google. A Local Radius Scan shows how your location is represented to Google searchers depending on where they search from. It sets a grid of pins over your area, runs the selected key phrase from each pin and records the rank your listing holds there.

Measure 01

SoLVShare of Local Visibility

The percentage of the scan's pins where your listing is in the top 3 for the selected key phrase.

The main number. Higher is better.
Measure 02

ARPAverage Rank Position

The average rank across the pins that show anything other than 20+. It tells you how high you sit at the pins where the scan finds you inside the top 20.

Rank 1 is the top spot. Lower is better.
Measure 03

ATRPAverage Total Rank Position

The average across every pin on the scan, the 20+ pins included. It shows how you rank across the whole area.

Counts every pin. Lower is better.
Why the top three

Below third place, the clicks dry up.

Clicks in Google's local results are not shared evenly. The first three positions take 18%, 15% and 13% of clicks, and positions 4 to 100 get under 1%. So SoLV counts only your top-3 pins.
