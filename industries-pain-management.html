The 2 KPIs that rule them all

For a pain clinic, growth comes down to two numbers.

Impressions and vanity clicks do not fill a treatment schedule. What decides whether a patient seeking relief finds your clinic, and stays long enough to book, is how fast your page paints and how much of your local map you hold. We work on both, every month.

KPI 01 · Speed

FCPFirst Contentful Paint

The gap between a tap on your listing and the first thing a patient in pain can read. 85% of traffic globally is from a mobile device, and a patient seeking relief will not sit on a blank screen. They go back and pick another clinic.

What turns a symptom search into a booked appointment: pages on SGEN CMS that paint in 0.9s on desktop. A slow competitor site we measured took 7.8s.
KPI 02 · Visibility

SoLVShare of Local Visibility

How often your clinic lands in the Google local top-3, everywhere in your service radius, when someone types "pain management near me," "sciatica treatment near me," or "chronic back pain relief." It tells you how much of the local map your clinic holds.

The gap between a booked first visit and silence: position 1 in the local pack earns 18% of clicks, and a clinic outside the top-3 gets under 1%.
